📱 Open AeroSense On Your Phone

You can use this app on any mobile phone (Android or iPhone) with full graph history and Bluetooth streaming:

Step 1: Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as this PC
Step 2: On your phone browser, open:
http://<PC-LOCAL-IP>:8000/index.html
Step 3: Install as an App (Add to Home Screen)

• Android (Chrome): Tap the 3 dots menu → "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
• iPhone (Safari / Bluefy): Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen".
(On iPhone, use the free Bluefy browser for Web Bluetooth support).

Step 4: Offline History is Saved Automatically

Every sensor reading is saved to your phone's internal storage so you can review trends and export CSV anytime!
