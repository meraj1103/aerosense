Problem Statement

[MOTIVATION]

Indoor and urban ambient air pollution poses severe health risks, contributing to respiratory diseases, cardiovascular stress, and cognitive fatigue.

  • Market Gap: Commercial air monitors are either exorbitant ($150+) or provide siloed readings (dust-only OR gas-only).
  • Cloud Lock-in: Most IoT solutions require proprietary clouds, high-latency subscriptions, and fail without internet.
  • Our Solution: AeroSense combines dual-sensor physical fusion, microsecond optical timing, and zero-install Web Bluetooth into a local-first PWA.

Dual Hardware Fusion

[SENSORS]
🔬 Sharp GP2Y
Principle: Optical scattering via internal IRED + phototransistor.
Target: PM2.5 / PM10 airborne fine particles & smoke.
Conditioning: 150Ω resistor + 220μF noise buffer capacitor.
🧪 MQ-135 MOS
Principle: SnO2 semiconductor conductivity modulation.
Target: VOCs, Ammonia (NH3), Alcohol, Benzene, CO2.
Power: Regulated 5V heating coil with ADC1 analog read.

Circuit Architecture

[GPIO / ADC1]
  • ADC1 Conflict Avoidance: Both sensors route strictly to GPIO 34 and GPIO 35, ensuring 100% ADC reliability during active BLE transmission.
  • Optical Pulse Drive: GPIO 26 triggers active-low LED switching with microsecond precision.
  • Status Indicator: Onboard GPIO 2 blue LED pulses in beacon mode and locks solid on connection.

Precision Optical Sampling

[280μs TIMING]

The Sharp GP2Y1014AU0F requires sub-millisecond coordination between the emitter diode and the phototransistor receiver:

1
LED ON: Digital Pin 26 driven LOW for exactly 280 μs.
2
ADC Sample: Vo measured at pin 35 precisely at the 280 μs mark.
3
LED OFF: Pulse completed at 320 μs, LED turned off.
4
Recovery: Rest period of 9,640 μs (10 ms continuous cycle).

DSP & Outlier Elimination

[ALGORITHMS]
  • Trimmed-Median Average: Collects 8 successive optical pulses, bubble-sorts them, and discards the top 2 and bottom 2 outliers to neutralize optical dust motes and ripple.
  • Standard Dust Characteristic Formula:
    Density (mg/m³) = 0.17 × Vo - 0.1
    PM2.5 (μg/m³) = Density × 1000
  • Composite Health Index: Dynamic weighted fusion between particulate dust and gas contamination.
